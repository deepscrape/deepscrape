Legal bases include performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where we ask for it.

4. Sharing and processors

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers who help run the Service: cloud infrastructure and hosting (Google Cloud / Firebase), payment processing (Stripe), third-party AI and language-model providers you choose to route data to, and analytics services. Each processor acts under our instructions and is bound by appropriate data-protection terms.

When you use the Service to collect data from websites you point it at, you are responsible for ensuring that collection is lawful and complies with the target sites’ terms and applicable law. We process that data on your instructions as a processor.

6. Retention

We keep account and usage data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You may delete your account and we will delete or anonymise associated data unless we must retain it for legal or security reasons.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring — to protect data. No method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions outside your country of residence through the providers listed above. Where transfers occur, we rely on recognised safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

9. Your rights

Where data-protection law applies to you (for example, under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can exercise these by contacting us through the contact page. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. Cookies

We set cookies that are strictly necessary to operate the Service: authentication cookies once you sign in, and a security cookie that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. These are required for the Service to work and are not used to track you.

Analytics cookies are set only with your consent, which we ask for in a banner on your first visit. If you accept, we set a cookie named gid for one year and store a record containing a one-way hash of your IP address, browser, operating system and device type. We use it to count unique visitors and to understand how the Service is used. If you decline, or do not answer, no analytics cookie is set and no such record is created. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site’s cookies in your browser settings; the banner will ask again and a decline stops further analytics records from being created.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under the applicable age of consent, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
